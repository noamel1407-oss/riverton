RIVERTON RESIDENCES sits directly on the riverfront promenade, minutes from the centre and open to the water on three sides. Five floors, private terraces, a landscaped forecourt and a residents' lobby staffed around the clock.

Full-height glazing across the entire western wall, a terrace deep enough to dine on, and the skyline turning gold every evening from your own sofa.
